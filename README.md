# F-15-Flight-Sim-
This is a flight sim where you fly around in an F-15 trying not to be shot by enemy planes and trying to earn points. You have to be careful. 
You can change planes, but the f-15 is the best for beginners and experienced players. To play the tutorial press 'T'. To play a game with the settings already enabled/selected press 'ENTER'. You have a selection of different modes and difficulties to choose from:

Easy: For beginners and those looking to play easy
Medium: For those who place themselves above the easy level
Hard: Those looking for some complex challenges

Free Flight: Destroy the red towers in the cities with missiles and bullets
Target Practice: Shoot targets before time ends
Navigation: Fly through the checkpoints
Combat: Engage in thrilling combat with a countdown

There are also some different planes to select:
F-15 Striker: An agile fighter plane with more missiles at your disposal
F-15: Eagle: A great mixed plane with 6 missiles and a bunch of cool stuff. (Ideal)
Heavy Bomber: A large, slow plane that is hard to maneuver and control can drop bombs with the 'V' key. (Hardest and least favourable)
